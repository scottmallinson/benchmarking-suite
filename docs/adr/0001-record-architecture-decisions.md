# 0001. Record architecture decisions

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-04

## Context

We need to preserve the reasoning behind significant decisions so that future
contributors, human or agent, understand why things are the way they are.

## Decision

We will keep lightweight Architecture Decision Records in `docs/adr`, following
[Michael Nygard's format](https://cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions).

## Consequences

Decisions are discoverable and reviewable in PRs. There is a small upfront cost
to writing each record.
